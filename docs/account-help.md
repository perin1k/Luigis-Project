# Staff sign-in and account help

For employees, managers and the owner. Start at
[Staff sign-in](https://luigispizzeria.duckdns.org/staff/sign-in).
Use your own account; do not share a login with the team.

## Sign in

1. Enter your **Username** and **Password**, then select **Sign in**.
2. If asked for an **Authenticator code**, open the authenticator app on your phone
   and find your Luigi’s Pizza entry. Enter its current six-digit number, then
   select **Verify and sign in**. The number changes about every 30 seconds.
3. Check your displayed role. Employee is regular staff; Admin is a manager;
   Owner has the business settings and sales reports. If your role seems wrong,
   ask the owner rather than borrowing someone else’s login.

You may be signed out after 30 minutes without activity or eight hours total.
Sign in again if asked. This does not mean your account was deleted.

## Accept a staff invitation

Ask your manager or owner for your username and private invitation code. The screen
calls that code an **Invitation token**. It works once and expires after 24 hours.
Do not post it in a group chat or public document.

1. Open [Create your staff account](https://luigispizzeria.duckdns.org/staff/enroll).
2. Enter the invitation token and **Choose a password**. Use a unique passphrase
   of at least 15 characters; avoid common phrases or your username.
3. Select **Create my staff account**, then return to sign-in.
4. New managers must set up their phone’s authenticator before gaining manager access.

## Set up a manager’s authenticator

An authenticator is an app that gives you the changing six-digit sign-in code.
It is different from a recovery code, which is a longer, single-use backup code.
You need an authenticator app installed on your phone before beginning.

1. Open [Account recovery and setup](https://luigispizzeria.duckdns.org/staff/recovery).
2. Expand **First administrator authenticator setup**. Enter **Setup username**
   and **Setup password**, then select **Begin first authenticator setup**.
3. In your phone’s authenticator app, choose its add-account/scan option and scan
   the QR square shown on your computer. Keep that square private. If scanning
   is not possible, use the displayed **Private setup key** in the app instead.
4. Enter the new Luigi’s Pizza entry’s six-digit code in **New authenticator code**
   and use the confirmation button. Save the displayed recovery codes privately,
   separately from your password. Each works only once; they cannot be shown again.
5. Select **I saved my codes — hide them**, return to sign-in, and use a fresh code.

This setup is only for a new manager invitation or an owner-approved promotion.
It cannot replace an existing authenticator. Initial Owner creation is handled
privately with website support, not by an ordinary staff invitation.

## Lost phone or missing codes

If you still have your password and an unused recovery code, open the recovery
page. Enter **Username**, **Password** and **Unused recovery code**, then select
**Begin authenticator replacement**. This signs you out elsewhere and turns off
the old authenticator. Finish the new phone setup and save the replacement recovery
codes before leaving; the old recovery codes will no longer work.

If you do not have a recovery code, forgot your password or cannot finish setup,
ask the owner/designated website support person for help. For the current project,
the screen directs you to Brody for an in-person identity check. Do not keep
guessing, create a second Owner account or send anyone your password, QR square,
setup key or codes. Website support has a separate private recovery procedure.

If a six-digit code is rejected, check that you selected the correct Luigi’s Pizza
entry and that your phone’s date/time is set automatically. Wait for a fresh code
before trying again. Ask for help if it still fails.

## Owner and managers: invite or reactivate staff

On your staff account page, enter the new **Username**. The Owner also chooses
**Account role**: Employee or Admin. Select **Create invitation**, privately give
the recipient the username and one-time invitation token, then use **Hide token**.
Managers can invite/manage Employees only. Ask the Owner for any Admin changes.

Use the account’s **Deactivate** action when access must stop, and read the
confirmation carefully. Use **Reactivate** for the same person when access is
approved again. Reactivation requires them to sign in afresh; it does not restore
an old signed-in session. The Owner account cannot be deactivated through ordinary
staff controls. Business ownership changes need a separate support review.
