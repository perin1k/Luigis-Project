# Your customer account

For customers using Luigi’s Pizza. You can still order as a guest without creating
an account. Customer accounts do not provide staff or manager access.
Choose **My account** on the website to begin.

## Create an account

1. Choose **My account**, then **Create account**.
2. Choose a username of 3–64 letters, numbers, dots, underscores or hyphens.
   Capital letters do not make a different username. Keep a note of your username.
3. Choose a unique password of at least 15 characters and select
   **Create customer account**.
4. Save the displayed recovery codes privately, separately from your password.
   Each works once; they cannot be displayed again. Select **I saved my codes — hide them**.

Recovery codes let you reset a forgotten password. There is no email reset service
in this version. If you lose your password and every code, the restaurant cannot
recover the account by matching your name, phone number or email. You can create
a new account, but it will not inherit another account’s orders.

## Save details for your next order

Under **Saved contact details**, optionally enter your name, phone or email and
select **Save contact details**. For this demonstration, use invented details.
Your next order fills them in so you can review and correct them. Editing checkout
details does not update your profile, and editing your profile does not alter past
orders. The order’s required contact fields still apply.

Saved profile details stay until account deletion or the project’s authorized final
data cleanup. Simply stopping the hosted website does not erase retained disks.

## See your orders

Orders placed and paid while signed in appear under **Your orders**, including
their current progress and practice total. Refresh the page to check for updates.
Only your account’s orders appear. Existing guest orders are not linked by a name,
phone number or email; use their original private tracking links instead.

History is limited to up to 100 recent eligible orders. Orders disappear when their
identifying information expires, normally seven days after the final outcome,
longer for unresolved cancellation/refund cases. For cancellation requests use
the private tracking link saved from your receipt, or ask the restaurant for help.
No money is charged or refunded in this demonstration.

## Forgot your password?

Select **Forgot your password?** Enter your username, one **Unused customer recovery
code** and a **New password**. Select **Reset password and replace codes**.
This signs you out everywhere. Save the replacement codes; every old code stops
working. Then sign in with the new password. Never send recovery codes to staff,
chat, public documents or screenshots.

## Sign out or delete the account

Choose **Sign out** when finished, especially on a shared computer. You must sign
in again after 30 minutes without activity or eight hours total.

To permanently delete the account, read **Delete your customer account**, enter
**Current password to confirm deletion**, then select **Delete account permanently**.
This removes the saved profile, password, recovery codes and links to account order
history, and signs the account out everywhere. It does not cancel/refund existing
orders or immediately erase their required records. Their normal privacy schedule
still applies. Keep any original private tracking links you need before deletion.

If saving fails, read the message. A stale-details message means someone changed
the account: reload before trying again. A busy/too-many-attempts message means
wait and retry. Never borrow a staff account to work around an account problem.
